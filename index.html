FORM 4 • MARKETING

The Marketing Mix

The marketing mix is a set of decisions a business uses to satisfy customers and achieve its objectives.

Learning outcomes

  • Define the marketing mix.
  • Explain product, price, place and promotion.
  • Apply the 4Ps to a Zimbabwean enterprise.

The four elements

01

Product

What the enterprise offers, including quality, design and packaging.

02

Price

The amount charged and the pricing method used.

03

Place

How the product reaches customers in shops or online.

04

Promotion

How customers learn about the offer.

Zimbabwean example

Tariro sells handmade jewellery at a market and through social media. She offers customised pieces, compares costs before setting prices, delivers locally and promotes new designs using short videos.
